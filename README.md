
## Legal & Compliance Maintenance

Before launching a new integration or business feature:

- Identify what information is collected.
- Identify who processes it.
- Identify whether cookies or tracking are introduced.
- Update privacy documentation.
- Update cookie documentation.
- Review terms.
- Review refund policy if payments change.
- Update legal dates where appropriate.
- Test consent behavior.

When changing website data collection,
analytics,
advertising,
payment processing,
third-party integrations,
or business services:

1. Review Privacy Policy.
2. Review Cookie Policy.
3. Review Terms & Conditions.
4. Review Refund & Cancellation Policy.
5. Review Disclaimer.
6. Update the last-updated date where appropriate.
7. Re-test affected functionality.
